---
name: About statistic visibility
description: Preserve shared Home statistics and independent About publication controls.
---

About must reuse existing Home Statistics records instead of copying labels or values. Selecting a statistic for About must not change whether that statistic is visible on Home.

**Why:** The user explicitly required one shared source of truth for statistic labels and values, with About visibility independent from Home visibility.

**How to apply:** When changing About or Home statistics, keep About selection on the existing record and do not use Home's visibility setting to determine About's published set.
